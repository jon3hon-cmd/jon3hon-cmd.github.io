# Personal Google Drive Upload Tool

This is a personal tool that uploads files to the owner's own Google Drive.

## Privacy Policy

- The app is used only by its owner (jon3hon-cmd).
- - It requests Google Drive access solely to upload files to the owner's own Drive.
  - - It does not collect, store, sell, or share any user data with third parties.
    - - OAuth tokens are kept only on the owner's own device.
